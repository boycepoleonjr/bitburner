## Q1. Exact BitNode multipliers (v3.0.x source)

### Takeaway
Each of BN8–BN15 hurts hacking in a different way. BN8 turns script-hack income off completely (`ScriptHackMoneyGain: 0`). BN9 bans cloud (purchased) servers and cuts server money to 1%. BN13 and BN14 raise the w0r1d_d43m0n hack requirement to 9,000 and 15,000 while scaling hacking level by 0.25 and 0.4. BN12 scales everything by 1.02^level with no cap. v3 renamed the `PurchasedServer*` multipliers to `CloudServer*`.

### Cited Findings
**Defaults and mechanics** (MULT unless noted):
- Every multiplier defaults to 1, except `DaedalusAugsRequirement` = 30 and `StaneksGiftExtraSize` = 0. ### Inferences
- **WD hacking requirement by node:** BN8 3000, BN9 6000, BN10 6000, BN11 4500, BN12 3000×1.02^lvl, BN13 9000, BN14 15000, BN15 6000. Combined with the HackingLevelMultiplier, the effective difficulty of reaching that level, measured as WD ÷ level mult (higher is worse), is roughly: BN8 3000, BN11 7500, BN15 10000, BN9 12000, BN10 ~17100, BN13 36000, BN14 37500. This is a rough guide only, because exp gain multipliers also differ: BN9 0.05, BN13 0.1, BN11 0.5.
- **Values at sample BN12 levels.** 1.02^lvl ≈ 1.02 at lvl 1, 1.22 at lvl 10, 1.49 at lvl 20, 2.0 at lvl 35, 2.69 at lvl 50, 7.24 at lvl 100. So BN12.35 (SF12.34 owned) roughly means WD 6000, half hacking level, ¼ server max money, and 2× aug money and rep cost. DaedalusAugsRequirement reaches 40 at lvl ≥ 117.
- Our cloud-server RAM manager (lib/cloud.js) has to read the per-node softcap. 1 PB (2^20 GB) costs 4^14 ≈ 2.7×10^8 times its linear price in BN8, 2^14 = 16,384 times in BN11, and about 720 times in BN13. Cloud servers are disallowed entirely in BN9.

### Gaps
- I did not trace every consumer of each multiplier. Examples: whether `HackingSpeedMultiplier` 0.3 applies inversely to hack/grow/weaken times (the field doc says it "influences how quickly … calls run"), and the exact Hacknet hash formulas for BN9.

## Q2. Victory condition, and whether hacking alone can finish each node

### Takeaway
Every node is won by backdooring/hacking w0r1d_d43m0n after installing The Red Pill (or by the Bladeburner route, which we don't automate). Our hacking-only stack can in principle finish **BN10, BN11, BN12 (low levels) and BN13**, and **BN14 and BN9** only very slowly. It **cannot finish BN8** without a stock-trading engine (script hacking pays $0), and it **cannot finish BN15** without darknet automation, because Daedalus doesn't sell The Red Pill there.

### Cited Findings
- General rule: join Daedalus, buy and install The Red Pill, then hack w0r1d_d43m0n ("3000, sometimes more"). The second method (Bladeburner) unlocks after BN6 or BN7. — [bitnodes.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnodes.md)
- The game treats a BitNode as finished when `w0r1d_d43m0n.backdoorInstalled` is set. — [BitNodeUtils.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNodeUtils.ts)
- Once the Red Pill is installed, w0r1d_d43m0n gets linked to the Daedalus server on the network. — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts)
- **BN8:** you start with $250M (`BitNode8StartingMoney = 250e6`, set on entering the node and again after every aug install), plus a WSE account and TIX API access. Shorts and limit/stop orders are allowed. — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts); [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx)
- BN8 income: "disables most normal ways to earn money so that you have to use the stock market… You need to have a good pre-4S stock market script; otherwise, it's a slow run… Hacking does not give you money, but hacking and growing a server that has a corresponding stock still influence that stock's price." Grafting (SF10) is called "very useful" because "Losing capital after each reset sets you back a lot." — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)
- `grow()` calls `influenceStockThroughServerGrow(server, moneyAfter - moneyBefore)`, so grow with the stock option moves stock prices. — [NetscriptFunctions.ts L46/L302](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/NetscriptFunctions.ts)
- Stock access costs: WSE $200M, TIX API $5B, 4S Market Data $1B, 4S TIX API $25B. BN8 leaves the 4S multipliers at 1. — [StockMarket/data/Constants.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/StockMarket/data/Constants.ts); [StockMarketCosts.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/StockMarket/StockMarketCosts.ts)
- **BN15:** Daedalus does not offer The Red Pill ("Remove TRP from daedalus in BN15"). — [FactionHelpers.tsx L204-207](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Faction/FactionHelpers.tsx). Instead the Red Pill is a darknet "labyrinth" reward. Each lab's reward depends on installed augs, in order The Broken Wings, The Boots, The Hammer, The Staff. In BN15, the lab you reach after those four is **EternalLab (depth 29, needs charisma ≥ 3000)**, and it grants The Red Pill. The earlier labs are NormalLab (depth 7, cha 300), CruelLab (depth 12, cha 600), MercilessLab (depth 19, cha 1500) and UberLab (depth 23, cha 2500). — [DarkNet/effects/labyrinth.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/DarkNet/effects/labyrinth.ts). (The multiplier field doc says "fourth lab", but the code path gives TRP at the 5th lab in BN15. Flagged as a minor inconsistency.)
- The comprehensive guide says BN15 "_can_" be completed "with a simple script that only solves a few of the basic server puzzle types to get passwords and copies itself around, but it will be much slower". Darknet servers are unstable, can only be reached from adjacent servers, and need passwords cracked via `ns.dnet.authenticate`/`heartbleed`. — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md); [darknet.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/programming/darknet.md)
- **BN12:** "Every time this BitNode is destroyed, it becomes slightly harder… There is no maximum level". Only the BN12 multipliers depend on level (`getBitNodeMultipliers(n, activeSF+1)`). — [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx). "In the first dozen levels, it's an easy one, and you can beat it with any mechanics." — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)
- **BN9:** the guide calls its multipliers "extremely harsh… disables private servers and significantly raises the RAM cost of your home computer… You must find a way to properly utilize HackNet servers". Inside BN9 you get the SF9.3 free hacknet server on entry. — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md). The code creates a hacknet server with level 100, 10 cores and cache 5 when entering BN9 or with SF9.3. This runs in `prestigeSourceFile` (BitNode entry), not on aug install. — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts)
- **BN13 and BN9:** "extremely hard… Bladeburner is a good choice in extremely hard BitNodes (e.g., BitNode 9, BitNode 13)". — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)
- **BN14:** "If you use IPvGO, even a slightly improved version of the tutorial script will help you complete this BitNode without much trouble." IPvGO is available from the start of every game (CIA in Sector-12, or `ns.go`). — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)

### Inferences
**Hacking-only verdict per node, for our stack (SF1.1, SF4.1, SF5.1; Singularity at 16× RAM):**
- **BN12 (first few levels): yes, as-is.** It is nearly BN1 (multipliers 1.02–1.2). The one hazard is BN12's `DarknetLabyrinthRewardsTheRedPill 0`, which doesn't matter to us because we use Daedalus. Daedalus needs 31 augs at lvl 1.
- **BN11: yes, slowly.** Server money is 1% and growth 0.2, so batch income is roughly two orders of magnitude lower. Weaken is twice as strong. WD needs 4500, with hacking ×0.6. Aug prices are 2×, so expect fewer augs per install.
- **BN10: yes, slowly.** Hacking ×0.35 and WD 6000 make it a long exp grind. Aug money ×5 and rep ×2 slow every install. Cloud servers are 5× the price and capped at 15 × 512 TB. Sleeves (buy up to 5 from The Covenant, BN10 only) and grafting are optional.
- **BN13: technically yes, very slowly.** The hacking level multiplier is 0.25 with HackExpGain 0.1, against WD 9000. The run starts with only $200k (PRESTIGE: `Player.money = CONSTANTS.TravelCost` = 200e3). Stanek's Gift is optional for victory but is the node's main helper.
- **BN14: technically yes, extremely slowly without IPvGO.** WD 15000 at hacking ×0.4, hack/grow/weaken run at speed ×0.3, and faction work rep is ×0.2, which hurts our faction-hacking-contract rep source. With GoPower ×4, a basic IPvGO bot is probably the cheapest unlock.
- **BN9: probably not practical as-is.** There are no cloud servers, home RAM costs ×5, server money is 1%, script hack money is ×0.1 and hack exp is ×0.05, and the free hacknet server only lasts until the first install. A hacknet-server/hash manager is effectively required, e.g. selling hashes for money or for "increase max money" / "reduce min security" upgrades.
- **BN8: no, as-is.** Script hacks pay $0 and every other income stream is 0. The only income is stock trading (shorts and limit/stop allowed) and stock manipulation through hack/grow. One helper: **FavorToDonateToFaction = 0**, so donation works at 0 favor. Money can then convert straight to rep at $1M per rep point × faction_rep mult, which removes the faction-work rep wall once the stock engine is profitable. Our "basic stock script" would need a pre-4S forecaster plus hack/grow manipulation, or a rush to buy 4S ($1B data + $25B API) from the $250M start.
- **BN15: no, as-is.** The Red Pill requires darknet crawling, password cracking, and four lab augs installed across at least four installs (if each lab aug needs to be installed before the next lab opens; `hasAugment` checks `Player.augmentations`, which is the installed list). The final lab also needs charisma ≥ 3000, which a hacking build doesn't train. Charisma comes from `phishingAttack` and the node's ×1.1 charisma level multiplier, and could also come from university leadership.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether labs 1 and 2 (`manual: true`) must be navigated by hand in the UI instead of by script. If so, BN15 cannot be fully automated. Worth checking in labyrinth.ts or darknet docs before committing.
- I found no source-level formula for the BN9 hash upgrade values.

## Q3. Source-File effects at levels 1/2/3 and what they unlock outside the node

### Takeaway
For a hacking-only autopilot, the Source-Files with the most direct payoff are **SF9.2** (start with 128 GB home), **SF12** (+1 free NeuroFlux level per level, no cap), **SF8** (+12/18/21% hacking_grow and permanent TIX), and **SF11** (cheaper aug price escalation, 1.9 → 1.767). SF10, SF13, SF14 and SF15 mostly unlock systems we don't automate yet (sleeves/grafting, Stanek, IPvGO cheats, darknet).

### Cited Findings
- **SF8:** L1 gives permanent WSE and TIX API, L2 shorts in other nodes, L3 limit/stop orders in other nodes. hacking_grow is ×1.12 / 1.18 / 1.21 (12, 12+6, 12+6+3). — [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx); [APPLY case 8](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts). On every reset with the SF8 feature, `hasWseAccount = hasTixApiAccess = true`. — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts)
- **SF9:** L1 permanently unlocks Hacknet Servers, which replace Hacknet Nodes. L2 starts home at 128 GB on entering a new BitNode. L3 grants a highly upgraded Hacknet Server on BitNode entry only (level 100, 10 cores, cache 5). Hacknet production is +12/18/21% and hacknet purchase/RAM/core/level costs are −12/18/21%. — [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx); [APPLY case 9](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts); [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts) (`if activeSourceFileLvl(9) >= 2 → setMaxRam(128); else if SF1 → 32; else 8`)
- **SF10:** "unlocks Sleeve and Grafting API in other BitNodes. Each level of this Source-File also grants you a Sleeve." There are no stat effects (APPLY case 10 is empty). Up to 5 sleeves can be bought from The Covenant, only inside BN10, and they persist across BitNodes. "The last Sleeve costs 100e15 (100q)". Sleeves keep progress through aug installs but reset when switching BitNodes. — [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx); [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md); [sleeves.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/sleeves.md). Grafting constants: cost multiplier 3, base time 3,600,000 ms. — [Constants.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Constants.ts)
- **SF11:** company favor raises both salary and rep by 1% per favor. work_money and company_rep are ×1.32 / 1.48 / 1.56. The per-aug price escalation multiplier becomes `1.9 × [1, 0.96, 0.94, 0.93][SF11]`, i.e. 1.9 → 1.824 / 1.786 / 1.767. — [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx); [APPLY case 11](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts); [AugmentationHelpers.ts L34](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Augmentation/AugmentationHelpers.ts)
- **SF12:** "lets you start any BitNodes with Neuroflux Governor equal to the level of this Source-File". The code pushes NFG at `level = activeSourceFileLvl(12)` on BitNode entry, before augs are recalculated. — [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx); [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts). The guide compares it: "When you upgrade Source-File 1.1 to Source-File 1.2, you get a buff equivalent to 8 levels of NFG." — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)
- **SF13:** Church of the Machine God appears in other BitNodes, and each level enlarges Stanek's Gift. Gift size = BaseSize 9 + StaneksGiftExtraSize + SF13 level (MaxSize 25). — [BN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx); [StaneksGift.ts L23](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/CotMG/StaneksGift.ts); [CotMG/data/Constants.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/CotMG/data/Constants.ts). The Gift "decreases many multipliers by 10%", and two CotMG augs remove that penalty. — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)

L2: charisma level boosts job salary and rep, and authentication is 20% faster. - Every Source-File except SF12 caps at level 3; `getBitNodeLevel` returns `min(sf+1, bn===12 ? MAX : 3)`. — [BitNodeUtils.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNodeUtils.ts)

### Inferences
- **Diminishing returns.** SF8 and SF9 follow 12 / +6 / +3 percentage points (the 12/2^i series). SF11's aug-escalation cut runs −4% / −2% / −1%. Each additional level adds about half of the previous one. The step changes worth getting are SF9.2 (128 GB) and SF9.3 (free hacknet server).
- **SF11 payoff for our aug planner.** In a 10-aug batch, the 10th aug's escalation factor is 1.9^9 ≈ 323 without SF11 and 1.767^9 ≈ 168 at SF11.3, about 48% cheaper. That gives more augs per install in every later node. SF11.1 alone gives 1.824^9 ≈ 223.
- **SF12 payoff.** Each level adds 1 NFG at every BitNode start. NFG's per-level bonus is not re-verified here; community knowledge is about 1% to most multipliers, compounding. Each BN12 level is slightly harder (2%/level), so the first ~10–20 levels are likely the cheapest per unit of benefit.
- **SF9.2** directly shortens our daemon-lite bootstrap phase in every fresh BitNode: start at 128 GB instead of 32 GB.
- **SF10** pays off only if we add sleeve automation. Sleeves doing faction work in parallel would attack the rep bottleneck, and grafting needs a separate planner. SF10.1 gives 1 free sleeve. The 5 purchasable sleeves can only be bought inside BN10, so to get them a BN10 run would need to make about 100q+.
- **SF15.1's permanent TOR** saves only the $200k TOR purchase (cheap). Its darknet-TRP path elsewhere (FinalLab, cha 4000) looks harder than Daedalus for a hacking build.

### Gaps
- I did not verify NFG's exact per-level multiplier in v3 source.
- I could not find the exact "Node Power" stat bonus formula for IPvGO (SF14).

## Q4. Special mechanics and restrictions

### Takeaway
Several of these nodes have traps that would break our current autopilot. **BN13:** installing any non-NFG aug before joining Church of the Machine God bans that faction (and with it Stanek's Gift). **BN8:** money resets to $250M on each install, which wipes stock capital unless it is held as shares (share holdings across installs are unconfirmed, see Gaps). **BN9:** no cloud servers at all. **BN15:** Daedalus has no Red Pill. **BN12 and BN8:** no darknet Red Pill.

### Cited Findings
- **BN13:** if you install any aug other than NeuroFlux Governor while not in Church of the Machine God, CotMG becomes `isBanned = true` ("CotMG is unavailable after accepting any other aug"). — [AugmentationHelpers.ts L75-78](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Augmentation/AugmentationHelpers.ts). The guide says "You must accept Stanek's Gift before purchasing any augmentations. NFG is the only exception." With SF7.3 you must also accept the Gift before joining Bladeburner. — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md). Entering BN13 sets money to $200k, shows the dialog "Trouble is brewing in Chongqing", and auto-joins CotMG when the Stanek aug is held. — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts)
- **BN8:** money is set to $250M on BitNode entry and also in `prestigeAugmentation`. The stock market is re-initialized on each reset. — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts)
- **BN10:** on entry, sleeves get shock ≤ 25 and sync ≥ 25, and a dialog points you to The Covenant and VitaLife (New Tokyo). — [PlayerObjectGeneralMethods.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/PersonObjects/Player/PlayerObjectGeneralMethods.ts); [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts). Grafting skips the reset and the faction rep requirement, but applies a debuff to many multipliers. — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)
- **BN12:** above SF12.100 the game shows the dialog "Saynt_Garmo is watching you" (flavour only). — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts)
- **BN15:** you receive the Darknet Handbook on entry. Darknet servers restart, migrate or go offline and kill your scripts. You can only interact with directly connected servers, unless you use a stasis link (in limited supply) or connectToSession. RAM is partly blocked until `dnet.memoryReallocation`. `.cache` files contain money, exp, programs or stock access keys. `phishingAttack` yields money or caches, scaled by charisma and crime success. `promoteStock` raises a stock's volatility. — [PRESTIGE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Prestige.ts); [darknet.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/programming/darknet.md)
- **Darknet in every node (v3):** "Added Darknet… Purchase DarkscapeNavigator.exe in the terminal to unlock access", and "The 'darkweb' server becomes a darknet server". — [CHANGELOG v3.0.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/changelog.md). The darknet and IPvGO are "_not_ locked behind" BN15 and BN14. — [COMP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md)
- **BN9:** you can run scripts on hacknet servers, but doing so reduces hash production. — [hacknetservers.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/hacknetservers.md)
- **v3 API breaks relevant to our scripts:** purchased-server functions moved to `ns.cloud` (#2367); nuke and port crackers return false instead of throwing (#2153); hacknet namespace RAM is now per-function (#2502); Stock API names were standardized (#2173); NS1 support removed. — [CHANGELOG v3.0.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/changelog.md)

### Inferences
- **Autopilot changes needed per node:**
  - BN13: a "join CotMG / accept Stanek before first install" guard, if we want the Gift.
  - BN9: cloud-buy disabled, plus a hacknet-server/hash manager.
  - BN8: a stock engine. Exiting positions before install vs. holding them across install needs checking (it is unconfirmed whether stock holdings survive an install). Since rep can be bought at 0 favor, the aug planner should switch to donation-based rep.
  - BN15: a darknet crawler, password solvers and a charisma plan.
  - BN14: an IPvGO player is optional but highly leveraged.
- Our existing "favor ≥150 → donate" rule should read `favorNeededToDonate()` (or the BN multiplier) instead of hard-coding 150. The threshold is 0 in BN8 and 150 × 1.02^lvl in BN12.

### Gaps
- I did not confirm whether stock positions survive an aug install in BN8. PRESTIGE re-initializes the stock market on reset, which suggests they do not, but I did not read `initStockMarket` itself.

## Q5. Completion times and recommended ordering (community opinion vs source fact)

### Takeaway
The in-game guides, which ship with the game, are the best available ordering source. They recommend leaving BN8 and BN11 for late, playing BN12 after all mechanics are unlocked, and preparing carefully for BN9 and BN13. I could not retrieve community-reported wall-clock completion times for these nodes in v3: Reddit, Steam and the readthedocs mirrors were blocked by the proxy or unreachable. No hour estimates are reported here.

### Cited Findings
- (Official docs, opinion) tl;dr: "BN10 has interesting mechanics if you can generate lots of money. BN9, BN13, BN14, and BN15 are tough but have interesting new mechanics… BN8 and BN11 are tough but offer little benefit to new players. BN12 is easy to start but quickly ramps up in difficulty." — [SHORT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_short_guide.md)
- (Official docs, opinion) BN8: "Completing at least BN10.1 is highly recommended in order to have Grafting… Having a good hacking script can also help in order to manipulate the market." BN11: "very difficult for not much in return." - (Official docs, opinion) "Bad BitNodes: BitNode 11 is hard, but its rewards are mediocre. You should only do it at the end, for the sake of completion." BN8: "Even with good scripts, it still takes a long time to complete this BitNode." BN10: "Most people complete this BitNode in one go to get 8 Sleeves" (3 from SF10.1–10.3 plus 5 bought). - (Community, 2022, pre-v3; seen only as a search-result snippet, page not retrievable) A Reddit "What BitNode should I do?" guide describes a hacking-focused route that runs BN5 and BN8 early. - (Community, 2022, pre-v3; search snippet only) A BN8 challenge thread describes tracking recent ticks and holding a stock while "it rises or falls 4 out of the last 5 times". - (Community example, not a record) A GitHub autopilot repo uses "BN12.335" as an example label. — [cyguin/bitburner-scripts](https://github.com/cyguin/bitburner-scripts) (search snippet)

### Inferences
**Proposed roadmap ranking for our hacking-only automation.** This is my synthesis from the source facts above, not a community consensus. Criteria in order: finishable as-is, then wall-clock time, then later-run speedup, then diminishing returns.

1. **BN12 ×N (start with ~3–10 levels).** Finishable as-is and near-BN1 difficulty early. Every level adds +1 NFG to every future run, and it is the only unbounded SF. Interleave more levels later; it is also the final "infinite" sink.
2. **BN11 (×3).** Finishable as-is (WD 4500, the lowest of the hard nodes). SF11 directly cheapens aug batches (escalation 1.9 → 1.767). The guide rates it "bad", but for an aug-planner-driven bot its reward is more relevant than the guide implies.
3. **BN10 (×1, then ×2–3).** Finishable as-is, but slow (hacking ×0.35, aug cost ×5). SF10.1 unlocks sleeves and grafting. The value depends on writing sleeve automation, and later BN8 needs grafting.
4. **BN9.** Needs a hacknet-server/hash manager first. SF9.2 (128 GB start) and SF9.3 (free hacknet server) speed every later bootstrap, so it is high value once the tooling exists.
5. **BN13.** Hacking-only is technically possible but very slow (WD 9000, level ×0.25, exp ×0.1, $200k start). Add the Stanek-accept guard before first install and Stanek charge automation.
6. **BN14.** WD 15000 makes hacking-only impractical. Build a basic IPvGO bot (usable in all nodes anyway) first.
7. **BN15.** Needs a darknet crawler, puzzle solvers and charisma ≥ 3000 for the lab Red Pill.
8. **BN8.** Needs a stock engine; script hacking pays $0. Do it after SF10 (grafting) per the official guide, and use 0-favor donation for rep.

**Wall-clock times:** no reliable community numbers were found. The node's own BN predictor (predictor v2) should calibrate from the first BN12 and BN11 runs.

### Gaps
- No community-reported completion times (hours/days) for BN8–BN15 under v3 could be retrieved. Reddit, Steam and readthedocs fetches were blocked by the proxy or DNS. Search snippets only covered 2022-era (v1/v2) strategy discussion.
- I found no community reports on how high players take BN12 in v3.
- I found no community BN15 completion reports. BN15 was new in v3.0.0 (May 2026).
