# BitNodes 1–7 in Bitburner v3.0.x: fact sheets for a hacking-only automated run

Scope and version: the game source was read on 2026-10-11 from bitburner-official/bitburner-src. The `dev` branch package.json says 3.0.2 and the `stable` branch says 3.0.1. For BN1–BN7, `getBitNodeMultipliers()` is identical on `dev` and `stable`. Compared with v2.8.1 there are only three kinds of change: renames (PurchasedServer* → CloudServer*, RepToDonateToFaction → FavorToDonateToFaction) and new DarknetMoneyMultiplier entries. The v3 numbers below therefore also hold for v2.8.x.

## Cross-cutting mechanics that apply to all seven nodes

### Takeaway
w0r1d_d43m0n requires a hacking level of 3000 × WorldDaemonDifficulty. Hacking level is logarithmic in exp, and HackingLevelMultiplier scales it. Daedalus requires 30 augs (35 in BN6/7), $100b, and hacking 2500 OR all combat stats at 1500. Bladeburner (the only alternative way to win) is available only in BN6/BN7, or elsewhere once you own SF6 or SF7. Singularity costs 16x/4x/1x RAM at SF4.1/4.2/4.3, and 1x inside BN4.

### Cited Findings
- w0r1d_d43m0n is defined with `requiredHackingSkill: 3000` — [servers.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Server/data/servers.ts). When servers are initialized, `server.requiredHackingSkill *= currentNodeMults.WorldDaemonDifficulty` — [ServerHelpers.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Server/ServerHelpers.ts). The server can only be hacked once The Red Pill is installed (same file, line ~342).
- A BitNode counts as finished when the World Daemon's `backdoorInstalled` flag is set (`isBitNodeFinished`/`finishBitNode`) — [BitNodeUtils.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNodeUtils.ts).
- Hacking level formula: `floor(mult * (32*ln(exp+534.6) - 200))`, where mult = player hacking mult × BN HackingLevelMultiplier — [skill.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/PersonObjects/formulas/skill.ts), [Person.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/PersonObjects/Person.ts) (line ~59–61).
- Daedalus invite conditions: `haveAugmentations(currentNodeMults.DaedalusAugsRequirement)`, `haveMoney(100e9)`, and `someCondition([haveSkill("hacking", 2500), haveCombatSkills(1500)])` — [FactionInfo.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Faction/FactionInfo.tsx).
- Bladeburner access: `canAccessBitNodeFeature(6) || canAccessBitNodeFeature(7)`, where `canAccessBitNodeFeature(n)` is true when `Player.bitNodeN === n` or the SF level is >0. It can also be disabled through BitNode advanced options — [PlayerObjectBladeburnerMethods.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/PersonObjects/Player/PlayerObjectBladeburnerMethods.ts), [BitNodeUtils.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNodeUtils.ts). Joining needs 100 in each of str/def/dex/agi. The NSA button reports "Bladeburner is disabled in BN-N" when BladeburnerRank is 0 — [SpecialLocation.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Locations/ui/SpecialLocation.tsx).
- Bladeburner victory: "You must complete all the BlackOps in order to destroy the World Daemon" — [bladeburners.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bladeburners.md). The final BlackOp is Operation Daedalus (n=20, reqdRank 400e3, baseDifficulty 80e3) — [BlackOperations.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Bladeburner/data/BlackOperations.ts). The comprehensive guide says Bladeburner "doesn't rely on money or hacking skill" and that "You can destroy WD after completing the last Black Operation" — [comprehensive guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md).
- Singularity RAM: `SF4Cost` returns base cost inside BN4, ×16 at SF4 ≤1, ×4 at SF4.2, ×1 at SF4.3. `destroyW0r1dD43m0n` has base cost 32 GB, so it costs 512 GB at SF4.1, 128 GB at SF4.2 and 32 GB at SF4.3. `installAugmentations` uses SingularityFn3 (base 5) — [RamCostGenerator.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Netscript/RamCostGenerator.ts).
- Every SF scales the same way. The level-n bonus is Σ base/2^i for i < n, so 1 + base·(1, 1.5, 1.75). Example: SF1 is 16%/24%/28% — [applySourceFile.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts). BN level = min(SF level + 1, 3) except BN12 — [BitNodeUtils.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNodeUtils.ts). For BN1–BN7 the multipliers do NOT change with level: `getBitNodeMultipliers(n, lvl)` ignores `lvl` for these nodes. Repeat runs of a node are therefore just as hard; only your SF bonuses grow — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx).
- Cloud (purchased) servers cost `ram × 55000 × CloudServerCost × CloudServerSoftcap^max(0, log2(ram) − 6)`. Defaults are a limit of 25 servers and a max of 2^20 GB — [ServerPurchases.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Server/ServerPurchases.ts), [Server/data/Constants.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Server/data/Constants.ts). v3.0.0 moved purchased-server functions to the `ns.cloud` API (#2367) — [changelog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/changelog.md).
- Donating to a faction needs favor ≥ 150 × FavorToDonateToFaction (`BaseFavorToDonate: 150`) — [Constants.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Constants.ts). Donation rep = amt/1e6 × faction_rep mult × **FactionWorkRepGain**, so the BN FactionWorkRepGain penalty also cuts donation rep — [donation.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Faction/formulas/donation.ts).
- Gangs: in BN2 there is no karma requirement. Elsewhere you need SF2 and karma ≤ −54000 — [PlayerObjectGangMethods.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/PersonObjects/Player/PlayerObjectGangMethods.ts), [Gang Constants.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Gang/data/Constants.ts). Outside BN2, a gang "will not be a way to destroy the BitNode alone" — [gang.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/gang.md).
- v3 BitNode advanced options (per run) let you disable gang/corp/bladeburner/4S/hacknet server/sleeve, override SF levels downward, and override intelligence — [BitNodeUtils.ts getDefaultBitNodeOptions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNodeUtils.ts).
- Project's own observed data (local repo, not a web source): BN1 destroyed 2026-09-26 (SF1.1). BN4 destroyed 2026-10-03 01:39 UTC (≈6.5 days in BN4.1 with SF1.1). BN5 entered 2026-10-03, and hacking passed w0r1d's 4,500 requirement on 2026-10-05 (≈2 days), after which it idled ~72 h — /home/user/bitburner/AGENTS.md, /home/user/bitburner/docs/specs/aug-planner.md.

### Inferences
- **Effective World Daemon requirement** (3000 × WDD ÷ BN hacking-level mult, i.e. the level needed at mult 1): BN1 3,000 · BN5 4,500 · BN3 7,500 · BN4 9,000 · BN6/BN7 17,143 · BN2 18,750. The automation already cleared 9,000 (BN4). BN2 and BN6/7 are about 2x that "effective" level. Exp needed grows as e^(L_eff/(32·playerMult)), so even with a big player hacking mult (e.g. ×30), going from 9,000 to ~18,000 effective needs roughly e^(9,750/960) ≈ 2.6e4× more hacking exp than BN4 needed. On top of that, HackExpGain is 0.25 in BN6/7. This is the dominant cost for hacking-only play in BN2/6/7. (Computed from the formulas above. Treat the ratios as order-of-magnitude, because player mult changes over the run.)
- **Rough hacking-income index** (ScriptHackMoney × ServerMaxMoney, ignoring growth/security): BN1 1.0 · BN5 0.15 · BN6 0.15 · BN7 0.10 · BN2 0.08 · BN4 0.0225 · BN3 0.008 (and BN3 also has growth 0.2 and aug cost ×3). This is an inference, not a game stat.
- **Cloud-server price multiplier at 1 PB (2^20 GB)** vs BN1: BN4/BN5 ×12.8, BN2 ×39.4, BN3 ×78.7, BN6/BN7 ×16,384 (softcap 2). A 1 PB server costs $5.77e10 in BN1 and $9.45e14 in BN6/7. In BN6/7 even 16 TB servers cost $2.3e11 each, so the RAM manager's cloud budget logic must cap size there. (Computed from the formula.)

### Gaps
- Bladeburner victory flow: I did not confirm in code whether completing Operation Daedalus immediately opens the BitVerse, or whether you then have to "destroy WD" from the Bladeburner UI. The docs say the BlackOps "destroy the World Daemon". The v3 changelog mentions "Prevent ending BNs through reuse of Bladeburner UI event handler (#2574)", which implies a UI button ends the BN.

## BN1 — Source Genesis

### Takeaway
No penalties (every multiplier is 1, WDD 1 → w0r1d needs 3,000). Already beaten once by this automation. SF1 is the strongest generic multiplier SF (+16/24/28% to ALL player mults, plus 32 GB starting home RAM). It is the best repeat target for criteria 1–3.

### Cited Findings
- Multipliers: `case 1: return new BitNodeMultipliers();`, i.e. all defaults: HackingLevel 1, ScriptHackMoney 1, ServerMaxMoney 1, AugCost 1/1, FactionWorkRep 1, WDD 1, Daedalus 30 augs — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx), [BitNodeMultipliers.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNodeMultipliers.ts).
- SF1: "start with 32GB of RAM on their home computer when entering a new BitNode and increases all of the player's multipliers by Level 1: 16%, Level 2: 24%, Level 3: 28%". The code multiplies hacking chance/speed/money/grow/level/exp, all combat and charisma levels and exp, company and faction rep, crime money and success, hacknet money, work money, and divides hacknet costs — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx), [applySourceFile.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts).
- Official guide: "Repeating BitNode 1 is the best choice… When you upgrade Source-File 1.1 to Source-File 1.2, you get a buff equivalent to 8 levels of NFG… Most people complete this BitNode in one go and get Source-File 1.3" — [comprehensive guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md). Short guide: "BN1.2 and BN1.3 are natural first choices" — [short guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_short_guide.md).
- Special mechanics: none ("It has no special modifications or mechanics") — [bitnodes.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnodes.md).

### Inferences
- Can our automation finish it? Yes (proven once). With SF4.1+SF5.1 and stronger SF1 it should be the fastest node. Formulas.exe is permanent from SF5, which removes one purchase.
- Marginal value: SF1.1→1.2 adds +8 points to every mult (1.16→1.24, ≈+6.9% relative). 1.2→1.3 adds +4 points (≈+3.2%). The 32 GB start RAM is only granted at level 1. Diminishing but broad.

### Gaps
- No reliable community wall-clock time for an automated BN1 repeat was found. The project's own BN1.1 timing is not in the files I read.

## BN2 — Rise of the Underworld (gangs)

### Takeaway
Hacking is crippled for the win condition: WDD 5 means w0r1d needs 15,000 hacking at 0.8 level mult (the hardest of BN1–7). Server money is ×0.08, there is no passive faction rep, and faction work rep is ×0.5. The intended path is a gang, which needs no karma in BN2 and whose faction sells The Red Pill. Our hacking-only automation could reach Daedalus (2,500 hacking is easy) but would struggle to reach 15,000 hacking. SF2 mostly unlocks gangs elsewhere and boosts crime/charisma, which is of little value to a hacking-only setup.

### Cited Findings
- Multipliers: HackingLevelMultiplier 0.8; ServerGrowthRate 0.8; ServerMaxMoney 0.08; ServerStartingMoney 0.4; CloudServerSoftcap 1.3; CrimeMoney 3; FactionPassiveRepGain 0; FactionWorkRepGain 0.5; CorporationSoftcap 0.9; CorporationDivisions 0.9; InfiltrationMoney 3; StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier 2; StaneksGiftExtraSize −6; WorldDaemonDifficulty 5. Everything else defaults: ScriptHackMoney 1, AugMoney/RepCost 1, HomeRamCost 1, HacknetNodeMoney 1, CompanyWorkMoney 1, CodingContractMoney 1, Daedalus 30 augs — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx).
- Mechanic: Slum Snakes, Tetrads, The Syndicate, The Dark Army, Speakers for the Dead, NiteSec and The Black Hand can form a gang; "in BitNode-2, it offers The Red Pill" — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx). In BN2 `canAccessGang` returns success without a karma check — [PlayerObjectGangMethods.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/PersonObjects/Player/PlayerObjectGangMethods.ts).
- Guide advice: "There is no karma requirement in BitNode 2, but creating a gang too soon is a mistake… Enabling territory clashes when your gang is still too weak is a serious mistake. You may lose all territory." The gang gives "access to most augmentations… This is arguably the biggest benefit of Gang" — [comprehensive guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md).
- SF2: "allows you to form gangs in other BitNodes once your karma decreases to a certain value [−54000]. It also increases your crime success rate, crime money, and charisma multipliers by 24%/36%/42%." In code only crime_money, crime_success and charisma are affected — [applySourceFile.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts). Farming karma outside BN2 "requires a significant time investment or Sleeves" — [short guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_short_guide.md).
- Community (opinion, anecdote): a Steam player reported clearing BN2 in "less than 2 days each time". An older 1.6.4-era guide calls BN2 "fairly easy, as hacking is still very profitable". Both are as summarized by a web search over [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1812820/discussions/0/798965318966919040/) and [Bitburner 1.6.4 docs](https://bitburner-fork-oddiz.readthedocs.io/en/stable/guidesandtips/recommendedbitnodeorder.html). I could not open the pages directly (DNS blocked), and they predate the v2 change to BN2 WDD 5.

### Inferences
- Version flag: the v1.x-era "hacking still very profitable" advice predates the current BN2 numbers (WDD 5, ServerMaxMoney 0.08). In v2.8.1 and v3 these values are identical, so the change happened at or before v2.x. Treat old BN2 time estimates as optimistic for hacking-only play.
- FactionWorkRepGain 0.5 plus passive rep 0 roughly halves the rep rate of our "faction hacking contracts" strategy and halves donation rep. With Daedalus needing 30 augs, aug-rep is the bottleneck. Then 15,000 hacking (≈18,750 effective) makes the hack-only finish very slow.
- For our automation, BN2 has low value until gang automation exists: SF2 gives no hacking benefit. If gang code is written, BN2 becomes much faster (the gang faction sells The Red Pill and most augs).

### Gaps
- No v3-era, hacking-only completion time for BN2 was found.

## BN3 — Corporatocracy

### Takeaway
Hacking income is the most crippled of BN1–7: ScriptHackMoney 0.2, ServerMaxMoney 0.04, growth 0.2, augs ×3 money and ×3 rep, home RAM ×1.5, cloud servers ×2 cost. The win condition is the normal w0r1d at 6,000 (WDD 2, 0.8 mult). Bright spot: donation unlocks at 75 favor. The intended engine is a corporation (seed money available in BN3). SF3 unlocks corps elsewhere (needs $150b), SF3.3 unlocks the full API, and it gives +8/12/14% charisma and company salary. That is near-zero value for hacking-only play.

### Cited Findings
- Multipliers: HackingLevelMultiplier 0.8; ServerGrowthRate 0.2; ServerMaxMoney 0.04; ServerStartingMoney 0.2; HomeComputerRamCost 1.5; CloudServerCost 2; CloudServerSoftcap 1.3; CompanyWorkMoney 0.25; CrimeMoney 0.25; HacknetNodeMoney 0.25; ScriptHackMoney 0.2; FavorToDonateToFaction 0.5; AugmentationMoneyCost 3; AugmentationRepCost 3; GangSoftcap 0.9; GangUniqueAugs 0.5; StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier 0.75; StaneksGiftExtraSize −2; DarknetMoneyMultiplier 0.4; WorldDaemonDifficulty 2. FactionWorkRepGain 1 (default) — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx).
- In BN3 the corp creation cost "can either be self-funded, or you can obtain the seed money" — [CreateCorporationModal.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Corporation/ui/modals/CreateCorporationModal.tsx). ### Inferences
- Our automation probably can finish BN3 as-is, but slowly. The income index (~0.008) is ~3× worse than BN4 (0.0225, which took ≈6.5 days with fewer SFs), and augs cost 3× money and 3× rep. The w0r1d effective requirement (7,500) is below BN4's 9,000. Expect BN3 to be among the slowest hack-only nodes. Gang (with SF2) or corp automation would change this sharply.
- Low SF value for this automation unless corp automation is built. The value of SF3.3's full corp API depends on that same future work.

### Gaps
- No v3-specific BN3 hack-only timing found.

## BN4 — The Singularity

### Takeaway
Already beaten once (SF4.1). Hacking money is heavily cut (ScriptHackMoney 0.2, ServerMaxMoney 0.1125, HackExpGain 0.4) and w0r1d needs 9,000 (WDD 3). Singularity is free (1x RAM) inside BN4. SF4.2/4.3 cut Singularity RAM outside BN4 from 16x to 4x to 1x. That is the largest per-run speedup for a Singularity-driven autopilot. Unusually, the SF has no stat effect, so the value is all RAM.

### Cited Findings
- Multipliers: ServerMaxMoney 0.1125; ServerStartingMoney 0.75; CloudServerSoftcap 1.2; CompanyWorkMoney 0.1; CrimeMoney 0.2; HacknetNodeMoney 0.05; ScriptHackMoney 0.2; ClassGymExpGain 0.5; CompanyWorkExpGain 0.5; CrimeExpGain 0.5; FactionWorkExpGain 0.5; HackExpGain 0.4; FactionWorkRepGain 0.75; GangUniqueAugs 0.5; StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier 1.5; DarknetMoneyMultiplier 0.4; WorldDaemonDifficulty 3. HackingLevelMultiplier 1, AugCosts 1, Daedalus 30 — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx).
- SF4: "Each level of this Source-File reduces the RAM cost of singularity functions in other BitNodes: Level 1: 16x, Level 2: 4x, Level 3: 1x". The applySourceFile case 4 says "No effects, just gives access to Singularity functions" — [BitNode.tsx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx), [applySourceFile.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts). In code, the cost is unmultiplied when `Player.bitNodeN === 4` — [RamCostGenerator.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Netscript/RamCostGenerator.ts).
- Guide: "you have to complete this BitNode three times in one go. Otherwise, you will have to pay the massive RAM cost. This BitNode's multipliers are a bit harsh, especially if you only have Source-File 1" — [comprehensive guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_comprehensive_guide.md). "it's strongly recommended to complete BN4.3 before using the Singularity API in other BitNodes" — [short guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/dev/src/Documentation/doc/en/advanced/bitnode_recommendation_short_guide.md).
- Project data: BN4.1 took ≈6.5 days (2026-09-26 → 2026-10-03) — /home/user/bitburner/AGENTS.md. The project's aug-planner spec already ranks BN4 second ("removes the 1 TB autopilot bootstrap wall… and the 512 GB destroy script") — /home/user/bitburner/docs/specs/aug-planner.md.

### Inferences
- Repeats of BN4 should be faster than the first run. Inside BN4 Singularity is 1x anyway, and the run now has SF5.1 (+8% hacking, Formulas.exe) plus any extra SF1 levels. The multipliers themselves are unchanged per level.
- Concrete savings from SF4.2/4.3: destroyW0r1dD43m0n goes 512 → 128 → 32 GB, and a 2–3 GB Singularity call goes from 32–48 GB to 2–3 GB. That removes the ~1 TB bootstrap gate mentioned in AGENTS.md. SF4.3 is the highest-leverage SF for this automation outside SF1.

### Gaps
- None material.

## BN5 — Artificial Intelligence

### Takeaway
Hacking-friendly: HackingLevelMultiplier 1, ServerMaxMoney 1, w0r1d 4,500 (WDD 1.5). The cuts are ScriptHackMoney 0.15, 2x starting security, HackExpGain 0.5 and aug money ×2. This automation reached w0r1d readiness in ≈2 days. SF5 gives Intelligence (persistent), permanent Formulas.exe, getBitNodeMultipliers(), and +8/12/14% to hacking chance/speed/money/grow/level/exp. That is a good, broad hacking benefit.
