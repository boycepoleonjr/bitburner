## Findings worth knowing
- On the simulated market the default estimator (half-life 20) passes G1 (79.7% vs 70% needed) and G2 (81.2% vs 75%) and earns a small positive edge net of spread; shorter windows are more accurate
  but churn and lose money net of spread. This is a simulation of the documented rules, not evidence about the live market.
- In BN8 each of the several aug installs needed for the 30-aug Daedalus count restarts the money from $250m. The BN8 plan (`bn8-readiness.md` §5.5) is therefore "rebuild per batch", not one climb.
- The earlier spec said `stocks.window` (40) and `stocks.impactSafety`; both are gone (`stocks.halfLife` 20, `stocks.maxTurnover` 2).
